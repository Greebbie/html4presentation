Session Loop

Most sessions have three clear beats.

01

Spot a route

A tower, puzzle, resource, or quest marker gives the player a near-term reason to move.

02

Switch the party

Element coverage and movement tools turn the route into a small team-planning problem.

03

Spend the reward

Artifacts, ascension materials, and characters convert exploration back into progression.
